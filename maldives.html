The Destination

One island each.
Choose precisely.

Every Maldivian resort is its own island, which makes choosing the entire art. Party of two or three generations? Seaplane romance or speedboat convenience? Slide-off-the-deck fun or barefoot Robinson polish? The nine islands on this list each answer differently, and precision here is worth more than anywhere else we sell.

Soneva Jani’s slides and observatory set the family fantasy; Cheval Blanc Randheli is the fashion-house statement; Velaa the private-island benchmark; Reethi Rah the celebrity classic. The Conrad brings the underwater restaurant that started it all; JOALI adds art, and JOALI Being the first true wellbeing island.

Fly the seaplane before three in the afternoon — the lagoons from a thousand feet are half the holiday.

Getting There

Ten hours, then
the seaplane.

London to Malé runs around ten hours direct in season, or one-stop over the Gulf year-round — the Dubai pairing exists for good reason. Seaplanes fly daylight only; we sequence arrivals so no one sleeps in Malé unless they choose to.

Speedboat islands suit late landings; seaplane islands reward morning ones. It is exactly the kind of detail that decides which island we recommend.
