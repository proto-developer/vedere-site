# Vedere website — implementation handover

**Date:** 19 September 2026
**Against:** *Vedere — Claude developer brief, 18 September 2026* (sections 1–11)
**Status:** implementation complete for everything that could be completed from the supplied
materials. Items requiring client facts, supplier confirmation, legal sign-off or new photography
are listed in the unresolved log at the end — they are **not** done and must not be treated as signed off.

### D. Legal, trust and compliance
1. **Privacy wording needs legal sign-off.** The replacement sentence is accurate and non-absolute but
   was written here, not approved. No retention periods or lawful bases were invented.
2. **No privacy policy page exists anywhere in the site**, so the brief's "link privacy information at
   forms and in the footer" could not be completed. ~60 enquiry forms still carry no data-use statement.
3. **Booking conditions are not published** and carry no version/effective date; no company
   registration number, VAT number or full registered office address anywhere on the site.
4. **ATOL 12703, TTA X3919 and the IATA accreditation claim** need verifying against current
   certificates, including the scope wording and any "100% financially protected" claim.
5. **Consent controls are not implemented.** The site loads analytics `dataLayer` pushes and the
   Trustpilot widget without consent gating. The brief requires consent to govern applicable tracking
   scripts; this needs a decision (and likely a consent platform) rather than a partial fix.
6. **"Issued in your name"** appears on 28 pages; which organisers actually personalise tickets needs
   contractual confirmation (Augusta National badge rules especially).
7. **Vedere Founders Club terms** state earn rates, point value and tiers but no expiry, redemption
   mechanic, cancellation treatment or referral terms.

### E. Testing not performed here
1. **Enquiry delivery test (§9).** Not run — it requires sending live submissions and confirming
   receipt in the real inbox/CRM. The form posts to `/enquire`; success messaging alone does not prove
   delivery. Must be tested on the deployed environment, including mobile and retry behaviour.
2. **Visual/browser QA.** No browser was available in this environment, so nothing was checked
   visually. Desktop and mobile crops of the new heroes, the text-only hotel cards, and the archive
   labelling should be reviewed on the test link before sign-off.
3. **Clean-path redirects and production indexability** (`.html` → clean URLs, staging noindex)
   require the hosting configuration and were not changed.

### F. Editorial decisions for the client
1. **"One Partner. Every Journey."** is removed from the logo as instructed, but still appears as
   editorial copy: the About hero eyebrow, the About `<title>`, twice on the homepage, and as the
   footer tagline on every page. Decide whether it survives as a line of copy.
2. **The homepage and Sports Travel page are near-duplicates** (same visible content, different title
   and description). Either differentiate Sports Travel or canonicalise it to the homepage.
3. **Stock imagery policy.** 163 illustrative stock images were removed as misleading. If you would
   rather show a clearly-labelled representative image than nothing while photography is commissioned,
   that is a different (defensible) choice — say so and it can be applied consistently with labelling.
