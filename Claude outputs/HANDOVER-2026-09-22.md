## 2. How the rates are shown (updated 23 September 2026)

Each card now shows a **seasonal range** for the entry room, e.g. "From £1,110–£2,720 per night · Varies by season ·
Classic Room · breakfast included · incl. taxes". Safari and Kenya lodges are labelled per person per night.

- **Range** (157 cards): low and high from prices actually seen for real dates across the next 12 months — the hotel's own
  seasonal rate sheet where one exists, otherwise 3–6 dated samples (Oct 2026, festive, Feb, May, Jul/Aug 2027) from
  Google Hotels / Booking.com. Seasonal hotels are sampled within their open season.
- **Single guide rate** (17): only one credible dated price could be found (e.g. Cala di Volpe, whose 2027 summer is not yet on sale).
- **On request** (16): no credible price anywhere, or withheld (The Sandpiper — only figure found looks like a suite rate).

Caveats recorded per card in `NIGHTLY-RATES-LOG.csv`: some lows are discounted / non-refundable rates; some highs are
understated because the entry room was sold out on peak dates; festive weeks were often not bookable. The ranges are
guide prices and should be refreshed each season or replaced with contracted rates.
