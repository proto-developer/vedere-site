## 2. How the rates are shown

Every price carries the date it applies to and what it covers, e.g. "From £2,290 per person per night — Green season,
6 Jan – 31 Mar 2027 · fully inclusive". Safari and Kenya lodges are labelled per person. There are three evidence levels, all in the log:

- **Official rate card** (15): hotel's own published rates for a stated season.
- **Booking-site price for a specific date** (113; Google Hotels, KAYAK, momondo, Mr & Mrs Smith): accurate for that date, but many
  are for late September / October 2026. **These will go out of date within weeks.** Replace them with contracted rates or refresh them.
- **Withheld** (5): Wequassett, Conrad Abu Dhabi, Excelsior Dubrovnik, Hotel Bellevue and Domes Miramare. Their prices were implausible or for dates the hotel is likely closed.
