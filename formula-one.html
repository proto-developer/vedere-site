The 2026 Season

Find your
Grand Prix weekend.

In Formula One, the venue changes the weekend completely. Monaco is a harbour-side occasion. Spa is a forest in the rain. Singapore is a night race in full humidity. Las Vegas is the Strip at midnight. Choosing the race is most of choosing the trip.

That is the part we spend most time on with you. If the racing comes first, look at Spa or Silverstone. If the spectacle does, Monaco or Las Vegas. Travelling as a family, Austin and Melbourne give you a city to enjoy between sessions. Getting that decision right matters more than any upgrade.

After that come the practical details that make a race weekend work — the transfer that leaves before the exodus, the hotel on the right side of the circuit, the restaurant booked six weeks out because everywhere fills. One specialist arranges all of it.

Ticket Guide

Where you sit changes
the entire weekend.

Four categories, and the right one depends on the circuit as much as the budget. We will tell you when the cheaper stand has the better view.

General Admission

Roaming access

Grass banks and standing areas around the circuit. The most sociable way to watch and the best for seeing several parts of the lap across three days — but no allocated seat, so arrive early for the popular spots.

Reserved Grandstand

A guaranteed seat

A numbered seat in a fixed stand for all three days. You see one sector properly rather than several partially, and you can leave and return without losing your position. The right buy for most first visits.

Premium Grandstand

The decisive corners

The stands that command a premium do so for a reason — a genuine overtaking zone, the pit lane, or a view of more than one part of the circuit at once. Turn 1, the main straight, or the end of a DRS zone.

Official Hospitality

Shade, food, a base

Elevated trackside viewing with a seat out of the sun, food and beverage throughout, indoor air-conditioned space and hosting. Materially different across three long days, and the right answer when hosting clients.
