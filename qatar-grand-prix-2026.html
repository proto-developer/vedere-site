The Race Weekend

Qatar Grand Prix
2026.

The penultimate round under Lusail's floodlights, a week before Abu Dhabi — which makes it the natural first half of a season-finale double. Doha's hotel and museum scene has quietly become world-class, and deserves a day or two of the trip in its own right. Booking terms stay flexible until the event schedule is finally confirmed.

Katara Towers guests are practically at the gates; city hotels 25–35 min. Private transfers on premium tiers; Doha Metro (Lusail QNB station + shuttle) serves entry packages well.

Every Formula One package ladders the same way. Grandstand Experience puts you in a named entry grandstand such as Turn 1. Premium moves you to the main grandstand opposite the pits, with start-finish, pit lane and podium in view. Hospitality Experience adds the circuit’s own named hospitality product. Luxury Race Weekend is Official Team Hospitality — inside your preferred team’s own suite, with garage visits, pit lane access and driver appearances.
